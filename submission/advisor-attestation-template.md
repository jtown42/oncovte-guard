# Advisor Attestation Letter — Template

**Student submissions to the AMIA / HL7 FHIR App Competition must include a PDF
of this letter, signed by the student's primary advisor.** Fill in every
`[BRACKETED]` field, delete this instruction block and the checklist at the
bottom, print on the training program's official letterhead, have the primary
advisor sign and date it, and export to PDF for upload.

The competition requires the letter to (i) identify the training program's name
and address, (ii) clearly identify the primary advisor, (iii) list all co-authors
and each author's contribution, and (iv) attest to the student's contribution.
The submission portal's Mentor/Advisor Statement additionally asks the advisor to
confirm the student's role in six components: (a) the text of the manuscript,
(b) the data collected, (c) the data analysis, (d) the literature review, (e) the
coding/development of the system, and (f) the evaluation and conclusions. This
template covers all of the above — items (a)–(e) below.
