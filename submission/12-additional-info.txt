Reviewers can reproduce every claim: `npm test` runs the full 185-test suite, `npm run build` type-checks and builds, and `npm run dev` opens the standalone demo with its live "what-if" editor. Key artifacts in the repository: VERIFICATION.md (the rule-to-source-to-code-to-test traceability matrix), submission/DEMO-SCRIPT.md (an eight-minute walkthrough of all five decision states, including a live threshold-flip demonstration), and docs/screenshots/ (the five decision states). No live-demo video has been recorded yet. The app has additionally been EHR-launched against the SMART Health IT public sandbox: loaded with synthetic oncology FHIR bundles, it completed the OAuth2/PKCE handshake, read live Condition, Observation, and MedicationRequest resources off the server, and returned the expected recommend, LMWH-fallback, and contraindicated verdicts - demonstrating standards-native interoperability with the reasoning intact (a public sandbox with synthetic data; not clinician-validated).
