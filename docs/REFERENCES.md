## Clinical practice guidelines and society statements

- National Comprehensive Cancer Network. *Cancer-Associated Venous Thromboembolic Disease.* Version 1.2026 (published 5 May 2026). [Primary source for: Khorana threshold and site list, excluded populations (VTE-2), contraindications to prophylaxis (VTE-A), ambulatory and inpatient LMWH dosing (VTE-B-1, VTE-B-2), DOAC renal/hepatic contraindications and GI/GU bleeding caution (VTE-D-5), chemotherapy-induced thrombocytopenia management (VTE-F), HIT options (HIT-B), prophylaxis duration (VTE-2).]
- National Comprehensive Cancer Network. *Antiemesis.* Version 2026. [Short antiemetic-course dexamethasone interaction relevance.]
- Anticoagulation Forum. *DOAC Drug-Drug Interactions.* 2026. [Perpetrator classification (combined P-gp/CYP3A4 inhibitor and inducer tiers), avoid vs. mitigate recommendations for azoles, idelalisib, enzalutamide, apalutamide, mitotane, tucatinib, cyclosporine, isavuconazole; BTK-inhibitor class bleeding flag.]
- Anticoagulation Forum. *VTE Prevention in Ambulatory Cancer Patients.* 2025. [Khorana application, original vs. modified score, RCC handling.]
- Beavers CJ, Rodgers JE, Bagnola AJ, et al. "Cardio-Oncology Drug Interactions: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association." *Circulation.* 2022 (published 12 April 2022). [Common metabolic enzyme substrate/inhibitor/inducer table; expert-guidance avoidance of apixaban with idelalisib and enzalutamide.]
- Ganatra S, Barac A, Armenian S, et al. "Diagnosis and Management of Cardiovascular Adverse Effects of Targeted Oncology Therapies: Bruton's Tyrosine Kinase, Immune Checkpoint, and Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor Inhibitors: 2025 ACC Concise Clinical Guidance." *Journal of the American College of Cardiology.* 2026 (published online 10 February 2026). Section 4.1.5 and Table 4. [Factor Xa inhibitors preferred over dabigatran with ibrutinib; direct thrombin inhibitors not to be co-administered; second-generation BTK-inhibitor bleeding risk. Volume, page range, and DOI unconfirmed — verify before printing an identifier.]
- Farge D, Frere C, Connors JM, et al. "2022 International Clinical Practice Guidelines for the Treatment and Prophylaxis of Venous Thromboembolism in Patients With Cancer, Including Patients With COVID-19." *The Lancet Oncology.* 2022. [ITAC; LMWH evidence in pancreatic cancer.]
- Patterson TF, Thompson GR, Denning DW, et al. "Practice Guidelines for the Diagnosis and Management of Aspergillosis: 2016 Update by the Infectious Diseases Society of America." *Clinical Infectious Diseases.* 2016. [First-line and step-down antifungal therapy for invasive aspergillosis.]
- Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes (KDIGO). "KDIGO 2024 Clinical Practice Guideline for the Evaluation and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease." *Kidney International.* 2024;105(4S):S117–S314. doi:10.1016/j.kint.2023.10.018. [Non-indexed eGFR at weight extremes for narrow-therapeutic-index drugs.]

## FDA drug labels

- Food and Drug Administration. *Apixaban (Eliquis).* 2024. Sections 7.1 (inhibitors) and 7.2 (inducers). [Avoid with combined P-gp and strong CYP3A4 inhibitors/inducers; itraconazole and ketoconazole named; rifampin, carbamazepine, phenytoin named; clarithromycin exempted.]
- Food and Drug Administration. *Rivaroxaban (Xarelto).* 2025. Sections 7.2 (inhibitors) and 7.3 (inducers). [Combined P-gp/strong CYP3A4 class avoidance; clarithromycin exempted.]
- Food and Drug Administration. *Dabigatran (Pradaxa).* 2025. Section 7.1. [P-gp inhibitor guidance; CrCl-gated avoidance; non-extrapolation statement.]
- Food and Drug Administration. *Posaconazole.* 2026.
- Food and Drug Administration. *Voriconazole.* 2025. [Strong CYP3A4 inhibitor, not a P-gp inhibitor; transaminase elevation frequency.]
- Food and Drug Administration. *Apalutamide (Erleada).* 2026.
- Food and Drug Administration. *Lorlatinib (Lorbrena).* 2026.
- Food and Drug Administration. *Carfilzomib (Kyprolis).* 2025.
- Food and Drug Administration. *Lapatinib (Tykerb).* 2026.
- Food and Drug Administration. *Doxorubicin Hydrochloride.* 2024.
- Food and Drug Administration. *Enoxaparin Sodium.* 2025. Dosage and Administration (Table 1, severe renal impairment). [30 mg once daily at CrCl <30 is an inpatient/medical-illness prophylaxis dose, not ambulatory cancer prophylaxis.]
